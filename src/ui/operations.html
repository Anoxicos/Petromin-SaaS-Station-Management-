M3 CREDIT AND INVOICING

Customers and finance

Use client-approved prices and tax settings. The app does not supply VAT rules, payment terms, or legal invoice wording. A sale and its later payment are separate records.

Customers and credit accounts
Post a sale

Fuel price fills from the effective station tariff at entry time; the server checks it again. Supply the tax code/rate only from the client's approved setup. Every tender must balance to the sale total.

Payment split

Credit payment schedule (optional)

Add installments only when agreed with the customer. Amounts must equal the credit portion. With no custom schedule, configured account terms create one due date. No interest or late fee is added.

Vouchers
Record customer payment

Record payment for an earlier credit sale; this does not create a new sale. Allocate only to the matching customer's outstanding credit sales.

Invoice profile

Enter wording and tax rules supplied by the client's accountant/legal adviser. Saving changes resets approval; only the client ACCOUNTANT role can approve.

STOCK

Inventory and dip variance

Fuel usage comes from validated meter readings. Dip stock is shown only when all station tanks have validated readings and supplied calibration charts. Variance is informational until operations sets a threshold.

Stock management

Owner and manager actions. Enter client-confirmed quantities, dates, currency, and reasons. Posted receipts and stock movements cannot be edited.

Fuel variance tolerances

Set a maximum absolute stock-versus-dip variance per fuel product using that product’s unit. No threshold is assumed. Results are informational and never block closing.

Client daily overview

View station activity together for the selected client. Exports include only stations and records available to your account.

Sales totals include posted app transactions by currency; cash/card inputs are reported separately and are not added to sales. DATA SETUP

History and opening balances

Upload an Excel-compatible CSV, review row validation, then explicitly commit. Imported history is an archive; it does not create invoices or sales. Opening balances append ledger entries. Paper records can be digitised into the same template. There is no configured historical date cap yet.

Download the CSV template, open it in Excel, then save as CSV or XLSX. XLSX import reads the first worksheet. Paper history must be digitised into these columns; compare the preview with source records before committing.

Browse committed historical records

Search the immutable archive by date, station, or record type. Results are paged; download the visible page as CSV.

STATION SETUP

Business-day boundary

Owner or manager

The cutoff defines which night readings belong to this station day. Choose the rule agreed with station operations. The app does not assume one for you.

METER AND PRODUCT SETUP

Station configuration

Owner or manager setup. Enter products, prices, equipment, and calibration data from client records. Tariff and calibration history is retained; do not create placeholder values for live operations.

Products and station assignment
Fuel tariffs

Enter an ISO timestamp with offset, for example YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss+01:00. Changes are effective-dated and audited.

Pumps, meters, nozzles, and tanks
Nozzle-to-tank assignments

Assignments are versioned; a later assignment records when the previous one ended.

Tank calibration charts

Copy the supplied chart points exactly. Use one depth and volume per line, separated by a comma. Drafts must be activated before they affect dip conversion.

Meter replacement or reset

Record a replacement/reset only against the verified meter index. The reset date and starting index must match the affected reading batch.

STEP 3

Cash and card reconciliation

Optional

Compare the reported amounts with the till count and card settlement. Variances are recorded for review and do not block closing by default.

Optional variance tolerances

Set only client-approved limits. Values use the currency entered for each reconciliation; blank limits are not evaluated. Tolerances never block closing.

STEP 4

Review and close

Not ready
